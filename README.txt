LIFE RESET TRACKER - iPhone app (works offline, data stays on your phone)

FIRST TIME
1. Put these files online once (free), for example on GitHub Pages or app.netlify.com/drop.
2. Open the web address in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
4. Open Life Reset from the home screen. After the first open it works with no internet.

UPDATING
Upload the new files over the old ones (same names). Then open the app once while online, close it fully, and open it again to get the new version. Your data is not touched.

Your data is saved only inside the app on your phone. Use the Backup section at the bottom to copy a backup and keep it in Notes.
